What we're known for

A little bit of everything, made properly

From your first cup of chai to a sizzler that lands at the table still hissing — every plate is built around the same idea: keep it simple, keep it generous.

Coffee & Chai

Cold coffee thick shakes, classic masala chai and everything between — poured slow, served warm.

Belgian Waffles

Crisp lattice waffles piled with Nutella, fresh fruit or our Death by Chocolate special.

Sizzlers & Mains

Chinese and Mexican sizzlers, loaded pizzas and pastas built for sharing.

Mocktails & Shakes

Blue Lagoon, Watermelon Chiller, Brownie Shake — cold, colourful, and photo-ready.

Our story

Two friends,
one small kitchen,
a big appetite for it.

BB Bites started as a joke between two "berojgaar boyzz" who loved cooking more than their day jobs. What began as recipes traded between friends turned into a menu, then a kitchen, then a cafe. We still cook the way we did on day one — fresh, unhurried, and a little too generous with the cheese.
